# Phase Progress

This document traces how the repository evolved from course proposal to replication package to extension study.

## Phase 1

- Phase 1 established the topic and research interest.
- The artifact is a proposal PDF in Phase_1_work.
- Its value is strategic rather than runnable.
- It records an early planned direction rather than the exact latest implementation.

## Phase 2

- Phase 2 built the modular replication baseline.
- It covered three datasets and the broad InstaSHAP workflow.
- It introduced the reusable module structure that later made Phase 3 possible.
- It also created reports, notebooks, tables, and presentation foundations.

## Phase 3

- Phase 3 narrowed the problem to one concrete limitation.
- The chosen limitation is unrealistic coalition construction under transformed-space zero masking.
- The implemented fix is empirical_background masking.
- The branch focuses on Covertype and multi-seed evaluation.

## What Stayed Constant

- The project remained focused on faster SHAP-style explanations.
- The repository remained centered on tabular data.
- Reproducibility and clear artifacts remained important throughout the phases.

## What Changed

- The project moved from broad idea to working package to focused extension.
- The evaluation became more targeted in Phase 3.
- The latest Phase 3 branch changed coalition construction without replacing the entire additive architecture.
- The repository accumulated multiple narratives, which now need explicit reconciliation.

## Presentation Framing

- Present the work as a progression from understanding to replication to targeted improvement.
- Emphasize that the final phase is strongest as an honest failure-aware research extension, not as a marketing claim.
- Show that the team knew how to turn research questions into code, tests, and artifacts.
